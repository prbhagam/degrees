# Demo video — flow and script

**Owner:** all four · **Added:** Sep 27 · **Length:** ~3:00 (a 2:00 cut is at the end)

This follows the PRD's demo arc (join → match → plan → feedback → circle), fitted to the app as it stands on `main` (c69983f). Button names in **bold** are the exact on-screen labels, so the narration matches what's tapped.

## Shot list + script

### 0 · Hook (0:00–0:12)

**Show:** B-roll of the HackGT floor, then launch the app on Phone A so the animated splash plays (the logo laps and ripples, then lifts).

> **VO:** This weekend you'll meet forty people at HackGT. A month from now you'll have seen maybe two of them again. Degrees is a dating app for friend groups. It turns the people you've actually met into real hangouts.

### 1 · Onboard, on Phone B (0:12–0:40)

**Tap:**
1. **Sign up** → fill the form → **Continue**. Speed this up 4× in the edit.
2. Interests: tap 3 chips → **Next**.
3. About you: type a Home base, then in *"Or just talk it through"* paste:
   > *CS freshman, into climbing and cheap ramen, looking for people to go to shows with. Not big on loud bars.*
4. **Generate tags from this**. Hold the shot while the tags pop in pre-selected, and *Loud venues* ticks itself under "Anything you'd rather skip?"
5. **Next** → Preferences → **Finish**.

> **VO:** Signing up takes a minute. Instead of filling out a profile, Charles just describes himself, and Gemini turns that into tags. It even caught that he'd rather skip loud venues. Every plan will respect that.

### 2 · Meet in person (0:40–1:05)

**Tap:**
1. **Phone A (Maya):** Home → **Host a meetup** → **Start meetup** → **Show QR code**.
2. **Phone B (Charles):** Home → **Join a meetup** → **Scan a QR code** → scan Maya's screen. *(This is the over-the-shoulder shot.)*
3. **Phone A:** the member list ticks to **Here · 2** on its own. Tap **Break the ice** and hold on the icebreakers.
4. **Phone A:** **End meetup** → **End meetup**.

> **VO:** In Degrees, a connection only forms in person. Maya starts a meetup. There's nothing to fill in, just a code. Charles scans it, and he shows up on her screen instantly. Gemini gives them something better to talk about than "what's your major." When Maya ends the meetup, everyone who was there becomes each other's 1st degree.

### 3 · Match (1:05–1:45)

**Tap:**
1. **Phone A:** **You** tab → **Degrees & preferences**. Hold on the three rows (1st only / **Up to 2nd degree**, marked RECOMMENDED / Up to 3rd), each with a real headcount. Then back.
2. Home → **Find my group**. Keep "Looking through your network…" and the pulsing rings in frame (3–8s).
3. The group screen: scroll to **Why this group**, then the member rows, which show **1st degree** next to **2nd degree · new** / *Someone new*.
4. *(Cut. Off camera, the other members tap **I'm in**. See prep.)* Come back as the count reads **N of N in**, and the bell shows *"Everyone's in — your group is on."*

> **VO:** Here's the dial that makes this different: how far out from you Degrees is allowed to reach. Only people you've met, their friends, or one step further. Those are real counts from Maya's network.
>
> When she taps Find my group, the server walks her graph out to the 2nd degree. It narrows the pool by similarity on everyone's Gemini embeddings, filtered by budget and distance, and Gemini picks a small group and says why.
>
> Notice what it doesn't do: it never names anyone Maya hasn't met. Strangers stay "someone new" until the group actually hangs out. There's no browsing and no swiping. Everyone accepts individually, and the group only forms once everyone's in.

### 4 · Plan (1:45–2:10)

**Tap:**
1. **Plan something**. This takes 10–25s, so cut from "Finding the best spot…" straight to the result.
2. Hold on the map, venue and price, then scroll to **Why this plan** and *Source: Google Maps*.
3. In **When**: **Propose a time** → **Tmrw** → 6:30 PM → **Propose** → **I'm free** → **Lock in**.
4. **Group chat**, then send one message ("see you there!").

> **VO:** Then Gemini, grounded in Google Maps, plans an actual outing: a real venue, at a real price inside everyone's budget, within everyone's travel distance, and with nothing on anyone's rather-skip list. They pick a time, lock it in, and use the chat to coordinate. The chat isn't the point, though. The point is to get off the app.

### 5 · Close the loop (2:10–2:40)

**Tap:**
1. On the group screen, tap **We met** on each person, then **Mark hangout as done** → **Mark done**.
2. **How did it go?**: 5 stars, **Great connection** on one person (call them *Leo* here, but use whoever you got), and **It was fine** on the rest. Then **Submit feedback**.
3. Home → **Find my group** again. Leo is back, and **Why this group** says so.

> **VO:** After they've hung out, Maya taps "We met" on the people she actually met. That's what makes them 1st degree. Feedback is one tap per person: great connection, it was fine, or not for me. That goes straight back into matching. Watch: she runs it again, and Leo's back, because she said she'd hang out with him again. Anyone she marks "not for me" never comes up again. Every hangout makes the next match better.

### 6 · Circle (2:40–2:55)

**Tap:** **Circle** tab → **Map**. Drag Maya's 0° node into a couple of people. The bumps flash, and you feel the haptic.

> **VO:** This is Maya's circle now. Everyone on it is someone she's actually met, and the green lines are friends who know each other.

### 7 · Close (2:55–3:05)

**Show:** the Degrees mark on the login screen: *"Real friends, a few degrees apart. No swiping."*

> **VO:** Degrees. Real friends, a few degrees apart.

## Where the AI is on screen

The Meta challenge asks for AI throughout, so it's worth knowing each time Gemini appears:

| Beat | Gemini call | Visible as |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | tag extraction from the paragraph | chips + the auto-ticked rather-skip |
| 1, 5 | embeddings (768-d) of every profile, re-run after feedback | invisible. Say it in the VO |
| 2 | icebreakers | **Break the ice** card |
| 3 | `formGroups` picks the group | **Why this group** |
| 4 | Maps-grounded planner | venue, price, **Why this plan** |
| 5 | feedback free text → new profile tags | invisible. Mention it if you add text |

## Prep before recording

1. **Production is on current `main`.** #37–#39 merged, so confirm Netlify was redeployed after them. Hit `https://degrees-api.netlify.app` before you record, or point both phones at a local `npm run dev`.
2. **Reset the demo state** between takes: run `supabase/scripts/purge_demo_users.sql`, then `restore_demo_users.sql`, then `npm run seed` (see [supabase/AGENTS.md](../supabase/AGENTS.md#demo-accounts-prod-vs-testing)). This touches the shared project, so tell the team first.
3. **Do one dry match first.** Run **Find my group** as Maya in a rehearsal, note who gets picked, and have those accounts logged in on the simulator ready to tap **I'm in** (auto-accept only covers Maya). If the take matches different people, accept as them instead.
4. **A fresh email for Charles on every take.** Signup rejects duplicates, so use plus-addressing (`charles+take3@…`).
5. **Grant camera permission on Phone B ahead of time**, so the "Allow camera" screen doesn't appear mid-take.
6. **Phone A's rather-skips and budget** should make the planner's constraints visible. Rehearse until the plan comes back inside budget.
7. **Enable Do Not Disturb** on both phones, and use a clean status bar (full battery, a round time like 9:41).

## If a beat misbehaves

- **Leo doesn't reappear in beat 5.** The boost is +0.25 on the next match, but Gemini's wording and its final pick vary. Re-roll, or cut to VO over the feedback screen ("…and that feeds the next match"). Don't fake it.
- **The rather-skip doesn't auto-tick in beat 1.** Tap *Loud venues* by hand. The VO still holds.
- **The plan stalls.** Tap **Keep going**, or **Suggest something else**. It never repeats a venue.

## 2:00 cut

Drop beat 1 (have Charles sign up off camera and open with him scanning) and the icebreakers in beat 2. Trim beat 3's VO to its first and last paragraphs. The feedback → rematch beat stays in, because it's the point: the app gets smarter from real use.
